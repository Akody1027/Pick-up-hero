How It Works

We believe in keeping things completely transparent. You shouldn't have to pay premium prices on every single grocery item just because you live outside the city limits.

  • Shop & pay on your favorite store's app (Walmart, Target, etc.)
  • Enter your order information into Pick Up Hero.
  • Our driver grabs your curbside pickup for you.
  • Your groceries are delivered fresh to your rural home.
